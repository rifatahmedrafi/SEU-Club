About the portal

One membership, every club

Register once and your details carry over to any club you join — no more filling the same paper form for the fourth society this year. Club leads see who's coming to an event before it starts, and members get one inbox for every update.

  • Single sign-up form works across all member clubs.
  • Instant confirmation, no waiting on office hours.
  • Built and maintained by student volunteers.
